## Description

Paint Anytime is a drawing app made specifically for Apple Watch. Sketch a quick idea or create a tiny piece of art right from your wrist.

Choose from a variety of brushes and customize colors, stroke width, angle, and opacity. Organize your artwork with layers, lock transparency to paint within existing boundaries, and use the Lasso to select, move, and rearrange parts of your drawing. Draw and hold to create cleaner shapes, or use the Fill tool to color larger areas in seconds.

Create custom colors with HSB and RGB sliders, organize them into palettes, and switch between color collections to match your mood. Explore different blending modes and see how colors interact on the canvas.

Save your drawings to your personal gallery and continue editing whenever inspiration returns. Use widgets to display your favorite creations or jump straight back into drawing.

## Features

- 6 brush presets with unique drawing behaviors;
- Custom colors, stroke width, angle, and opacity;
- Layers with transparency lock;
- Lasso tool for arranging your artwork;
- Shape recognition with draw-and-hold;
- Fill tool for coloring enclosed areas;
- Multiple color blending modes;
- Custom color palettes with RGB and HSB editors;
- Canvas zoom and expandable drawing space;
- Undo, Redo, and two types of erasers;
- Personal gallery with editable saved drawings;
- Apple Watch widgets for artwork and quick access;
- Compact, interactive drawing tutorial;
- Sharing creations through iMessage or email.

## What's New in 1.2.0

### New Features

- New Pencil and Crayon brushes to better express the texture;
- Blur brush for softening edges and better gradient;
- iPhone companion app to display, upload, download, delete, and share projects;
- Updates gallery animations to optimize for more content, and it is ported to iPhone;
- Autosave to make you less worry about battery drain;
- Rotate, crop and change Canvas size;
- Canvas Editor lets you choose and arrange on-canvas controls;
- Shape recognition understands more shapes, including clouds;
- Leading lines to easier draw the perspective images;
- Tutorial is separated on levels to get into the business faster (atwork);
- Allows to change your pictures from your gallery on your watch (atwork);
- Lets you put other image new by Canvas as reference (atwork);
- Apple Intelligence turns your sketch into picture.

### Bug Fixes

- Drawing area now extends under the system clock;
- Faster Undo and Redo.
- Faster Fill tool.
- Smoother movement of objects selected with the Lasso.
- Much smoother zoom in the gallery, without freezes, lag or jumps between grid sizes.
- Fixed mistakes in the tutorial.

## Requirements

- watchOS 10.0 or later
- iOS 17 or later
- iOS 18.2 or later for Apple Intelligence feature 
- Xcode with watchOS development support for building from source

## Build from Source

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/denis-kolchev/Paint-Anytime.git
```

Open the project in Xcode, select your Apple Developer Team under **Signing & Capabilities**, select an Apple Watch or watchOS Simulator as the destination, and build the project.

Depending on your signing configuration, you may need to use your own Bundle Identifier.
