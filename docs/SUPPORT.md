# Paint Anytime Support

Paint Anytime 1.0.1 is a drawing app for Apple Watch running watchOS 10.0 or later.

## Using the app

- **Learn the controls:** open **More → Tutorial**. The tutorial uses practice drawings and restores your own canvas and gallery when it ends.
- **Find saved drawings:** open **More → Gallery**. Save your canvas before switching to another drawing if you want to keep your changes.
- **Change language:** open **More → Language** and choose a language by its native name.
- **Share a drawing:** use the sharing action for a saved drawing. Available destinations depend on your watch and its installed apps.
- **Adjust tool settings:** open the tool button. To share width or color between tools, open **More → Tool synchronization**.

Automatic transfer to iPhone Photos is not available in version 1.0.1. Saved drawings remain in the watch gallery unless you share them yourself.

## Report a problem

Email [paintanytime.app@gmail.com](mailto:paintanytime.app@gmail.com), or use **More → Report a bug** in the app.

Please include:

- Your Apple Watch model and watchOS version.
- The Paint Anytime version and build number, if known.
- What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
- A screenshot or short recording if it helps explain the issue.

The in-app action opens an email draft; it does not automatically attach drawings or diagnostic logs. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting if you need to keep local drawings.
