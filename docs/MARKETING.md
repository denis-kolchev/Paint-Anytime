# Paint Anytime

Turn a moment of inspiration into a drawing, right on your Apple Watch.

Draw with Monoline, Pen, Marker, Fountain pen, and Reed pen. Adjust the color and width, experiment with the reed nib angle, and refine your work with pixel or object erasing. Undo and redo make it easy to try another approach.

Use the Digital Crown to zoom, move around the canvas, and save your drawings in a gallery on your watch. Reopen a drawing to keep working or share it using the options available on your device.

An interactive tutorial introduces the tools using a separate practice canvas and gallery. Choose your interface language during setup or change it later in the app.

No account is required. Drawings and preferences are stored locally, with no advertising or third-party analytics.

Requires watchOS 10.0 or later. Version 1.0.1 introduces an updated Apple Watch app icon.
